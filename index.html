About Us

PrimePepper
Global (Pvt) Ltd

PrimePepper Global is a Sri Lanka-based producer and exporter of premium-grade Organic Black Pepper Essential Oil. We combine the rich spice heritage of Ceylon with modern steam distillation to deliver world-class quality.

Our founder S.M. Sudesh Madushanka brings 19+ years of professional experience and direct knowledge of UAE and Middle East markets — ensuring we understand exactly what international buyers need.

We are committed to sustainable, ethical sourcing from certified organic Sri Lankan farms, delivering pure, natural pepper oil from farm to global markets.
