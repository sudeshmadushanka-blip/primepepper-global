What We Do Every Day

We harness the power of steam distillation to extract the purest black pepper essential oil. Our organic peppercorns are meticulously sourced from local Sri Lankan farmers in Welimada & Peradeniya — ensuring a product free from chemicals and full of natural potency.

Every batch is ITI-verified, every shipment fully documented, every buyer relationship built on trust.

Our Commitment

At PrimePepper Global, quality is not a promise — it is our process. From the moment peppercorns are harvested in Sri Lanka's highland farms to the moment our essential oil reaches your hands, every step is guided by transparency, science, and respect for the land that makes it possible.
